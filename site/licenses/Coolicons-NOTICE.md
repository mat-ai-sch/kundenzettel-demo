Nine original SVG icons are included as symbols. Symbol IDs were shortened and duplicated internal IDs removed; geometry was not changed. The UI renders them at 20 px instead of the source 24-px grid. Inline symbols duplicate the external sprite to support opening the kit directly from disk.

The product's `apps/web/src/components/AppIcon.tsx` uses 17 original Outline icons
from the same pinned upstream revision. Geometry is unchanged; group/element IDs
were removed and attribute names converted to JSX. The component renders native
inline SVG at 16, 20 or 24 px, with currentColor inherited from its control.
